# PeakCut Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 19, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

PeakCut is a video clipping and social media publishing application. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you use PeakCut.

## 2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, PeakCut may process:

- Account information, such as your username and login credentials.
- Videos and media files you upload or process.
- Social media account authorization tokens when you connect supported platforms.
- Content and publishing information needed to post videos.
- Technical information, such as application logs and error details.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Operate and maintain PeakCut.
- Authenticate users and protect accounts.
- Process and edit video files.
- Connect to social media platforms when authorized.
- Publish content when you request it.
- Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.

## 4. Where Information Is Processed

PeakCut is designed to run on a server operated by its owner or administrator.

Information may be processed and stored on that server. The server's operator is responsible for its security, configuration, and access controls.

If PeakCut is hosted by another provider, that provider may also process information under its own policies.

## 5. Third-Party Services

PeakCut may integrate with third-party platforms such as:

- YouTube
- TikTok
- Instagram

When you connect these services, information may be exchanged with them to provide requested features. Their use of information is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

PeakCut does not control the privacy practices of third-party platforms.

## 6. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect account information, media files, and authorization credentials. However, no internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

Users and server administrators should protect passwords and avoid sharing access to their PeakCut server.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may remain on the PeakCut server for as long as necessary to provide its features or as configured by the server administrator.

For questions about accessing or deleting information, contact the person operating your PeakCut server.

## 8. Children's Privacy

PeakCut is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly intend to collect personal information from children under 13.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PeakCut's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
